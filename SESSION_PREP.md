# Prepare and run a session

From **Overview** or **Sessions**, choose **Prepare session**. Preparation saves automatically with the campaign workspace. It works locally across supported game systems, with your own material and optional connected references.

## Assemble the session

1. Write the **opening situation**: where play begins, what is happening, and what asks for the characters' attention.
2. Set the **time budget** for the session. Scene estimates help leave room for breaks, discussion, and unexpected decisions.
3. Add **scenes**. Give each a title, type, time estimate, playable details or pressure, and a decision or question. Move scenes up or down as your preparation develops.
4. Pin the campaign records you expect to need. The references point to your existing characters, places, quests, arcs, and journal entries.
5. Prepare **clues and revelations**, **clocks**, and **character spotlight opportunities**. These are material to bring into play as circumstances allow.
6. Add your own **prep tasks** and check them off as you complete them.

The preparation checks reflect what you have entered. They help you spot gaps and an overfilled time budget; your judgment determines what the table needs. A brief session can use only the sections that help.

Your existing session notes and possible story directions remain available alongside the structured plan. Preparing a session leaves its campaign record and play status intact.

## Take the plan to the table

Choose **GM Markdown packet** to download the prepared material as a readable `.md` file. The packet is labeled **GM only** and includes private preparation. Store or print it using your preferred Markdown application.

Start the session from the preparation workspace or Sessions. The first start copies the opening, scenes, pins, clues, clocks, and spotlight opportunities into the Live Session Desk. Scene details and decisions remain visible while playing.

The live desk keeps its own progress. Resuming it preserves checked scenes, discovered clues, clocks, scratchpad, and log. Later edits to preparation remain in the plan; the current desk continues from its saved state.

Choose **End session** when play has finished, then review the Consequence Inbox. Approve the campaign changes you want to apply. Preparation is GM material and remains outside Player preview.

## Keep preparation recoverable

Preparation travels with the full campaign workspace, including its backups and an approved Archivist refresh. Stable session references keep the plan and desk attached when the session is renamed. Legacy references are recovered when the corresponding session can be identified uniquely.

Check the save status before closing. Use the workspace backup/export controls for a complete recovery copy; the Markdown packet is a reading copy of the selected session.
