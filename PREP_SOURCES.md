# PDF pages in preparation and play

Version 1.15.0 connects your imported reference PDFs to session drafting, prepared scenes, GM packets, and the live desk. Searchable page text already lives in the campaign's local reference library; these links use that saved material.

## Build a session from source passages

In **Build from notes**, search for a book title or words on a page. The source picker lists the book title, PDF page number, and a short identity suffix. Choose the page and **Add selected source**. Review or shorten its selected excerpt, then shape a manual draft or use the existing optional AI connection.

The same twelve-source and 6,000-character excerpt limits apply. Long pages initially select their first 6,000 characters; expand **Current record & source tools** to find another exact passage. Quotes in draft pieces must match the selected excerpt. Suggested additions remain separate and require GM review.

AI requests contain the selected excerpts shown in the workbench, along with the already described campaign/session titles, game system, time budget, and brief. Other pages and unselected text stay outside that request. Explicitly selecting a page here is separate from the library's **Use in GM context** setting, which controls automatic retrieval for GM inquiry.

## Keep a page at hand

In **Prepare session**, use **At hand for this scene** or the general pinned-record search to find and link a page. Approved note pieces also attach their cited pages when the pin option is selected. Two pages from one book remain separate references.

First play copies these references into the live desk. Open a scene link or a pin to read the page text beside the situation and live notes. The desk's pin search also finds page text during play. Linking, reading, and copying prepared material preserve the source document. GM packets include the full extracted text of linked pages; player preview excludes the private preparation and source library.

Unfinished scenes and selected pins retain their page links when brought forward into another session. Reusable templates continue to capture general structure without campaign-specific book references.

## Identity and changed sources

A link identifies the original imported book and its exact PDF page. Book renames retain the link. Deleted books, missing pages, duplicate identities, and ambiguous page numbers show as unavailable; a same-title replacement keeps its own identity. Reimporting creates a new book, so relink it deliberately.

Changing the selected page invalidates an unapplied notes draft until you refresh the source and review its quotes. Changes to another page leave that draft valid. Approved preparation retains the quoted text it used, while the live reader and GM packet show the currently saved linked page.

Page numbers refer to the PDF's physical page order and may differ from printed numbering. Legacy imports retain **page unavailable** labels; they do not invent a numbered page. Pages with no selectable text can be linked, and the reader explains the need for a text-based or OCR-processed import. A blank page cannot become a blank drafting source.

- Retain exact page identity and quotes through normalization, saved workspaces, approval, first play, GM export, and carry-forward.
- Send only explicitly selected excerpts to the optional AI endpoint, retain source-change checks, and keep GM material outside player preview.
- Exercise real PDF import, browser/offline reload, Windows process restart, narrow layouts, and current record-pin and notes workflows.
- Publish passing Windows checks, matching runtime assets, a versioned portable download, and valid update feeds.

These references display extracted text. The original page layout, diagrams, and scanned imagery remain in the source PDF. Real table use and additional-device coverage remain pilot work.
